# Indie Hackers

**Title:** Launched Quote and Sign: open-source proposal software with flat pricing

**Body:**

The bet: proposal tools price like sales software (per seat, per document), but most people who send proposals are freelancers and small studios sending a few a month. Flat pricing plus open source should win that segment.

What shipped on day one:
- Editor with a live pricing table; client toggles options and accepts on their phone; signed PDF and a verifiable record for both sides.
- Free (three live proposals), Pro $19 a month, Business $59 with ten seats and signed webhooks for CRM automation.
- The whole product on Cloudflare's free tier, so hosting cost is zero until it is not, and the code is AGPL so anyone can run it.

What I am watching: trial to paid conversion, and whether "open source" shows up in why people choose it or is just something they like to hear.

quoteandsign.com. Ask me anything about the stack or the pricing decision.
