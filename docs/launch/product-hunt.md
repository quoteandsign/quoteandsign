## First comment (maker)

Hi Product Hunt.

Quote and Sign exists because proposal software has drifted toward sales-floor pricing: per user, per document, per integration. A freelancer sending four proposals a month pays the same seat price as an enterprise rep.

What it does:
- You write the proposal in a clean editor with a live pricing table.
- Your client gets a link, not a PDF. They toggle optional items, change quantities within limits you set, watch the total update, type their name and tap Accept. It works on a phone.
- Both of you get the signed PDF by email plus a record page with a fingerprint of exactly what was accepted.
- Business accounts get signed webhooks, so accepted proposals land in your CRM through Zapier, Make or n8n.

Pricing: free for three live proposals, Pro $19 a month billed yearly, Business $59 with ten seats. No per-document fees. Fourteen-day Pro trial, no card.

It is open source under the AGPL and runs on a free Cloudflare account if you would rather host it yourself.

I would love to hear what would make you switch from whatever you use today.
